# Business Travel Policy

**Version:** 2.3
**Owner:** Finance

## Booking

All flights must be booked through the company travel desk at least **10 days** before departure. Bookings made later need approval from a department head.

## Daily allowance

The daily allowance depends on the city. Receipts are not required for the allowance.

| City class | Examples | Meals per day | Local transport per day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metro | Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru | ₹1,500 | ₹800 |
| Tier 2 | Pune, Jaipur, Kochi | ₹1,000 | ₹500 |

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 per night elsewhere.

Alcohol and minibar charges are not reimbursed.

## Claims

Expense claims must be filed within **30 days** of returning from a trip. Claims filed after that are rejected.
