REMOTE WORK POLICY

Version 1.1. Owner: People Operations.

1. Eligibility
Employees who have finished their probation may work remotely for up to 2 days each week. Remote days must be agreed with the manager in advance.

2. Working from another country
Working from outside India is not allowed without written approval from Legal.

3. Equipment
3.1 Laptops
The company laptop is the only device allowed for remote work.

3.2 Monitors
Employees may borrow one monitor from the office. It must be returned within 15 days of leaving the company.
